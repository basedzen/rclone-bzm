# Privacy policy for rclone-bzm

Last updated: 14 October 2026

rclone-bzm is a private tool. It is an OAuth client that lets the owner's own rclone installation read and write files in the owner's own Google Drive account, in order to mount and back up that Drive on a server the owner controls.

- **Data accessed:** only files in the Google Drive account of the person who authorised the app (the owner).
- **Use of data:** files are copied between that Drive and the owner's own storage. Nothing is analysed, profiled or used for advertising.
- **Sharing:** no data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to anyone else. The app has no other users.
- **Storage:** the OAuth token is kept in an encrypted secrets file on the owner's server.
- **Revoking access:** the owner can revoke the app at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
